Slide 02 — Overview

What are heart diseases?

A group of conditions that affect the heart's structure and function — restricting blood flow, disturbing rhythm, or weakening the muscle itself.

🩸

Coronary Artery Disease

Plaque narrows the arteries feeding the heart — the most common type and the main cause of heart attacks.

💗

Arrhythmia

An irregular heartbeat — too fast, too slow, or erratic — that can disrupt how blood is pumped.

🫀

Heart Failure

The muscle becomes too weak or stiff to pump enough blood to meet the body's needs.
